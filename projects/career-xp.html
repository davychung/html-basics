Overview

Career XP is a career development tracking system designed to make professional growth visible and measurable. It tracks real-world accomplishments, projects, skills and milestones as progression, using game-inspired systems such as experience points, levels and achievements.

The goal is not to decide what career someone should pursue, but to make the progress they are already making easier to understand. By connecting that progression to tangible evidence of work, Career XP can eventually provide a clearer picture of how someone's skills and experience have developed over time.

Why

As I entered my final year of university, I found myself asking what the last three years of studying had actually added up to. I had courses, projects and experiences, but no clear idea of how they were moving me towards becoming a professional software engineer.

Career XP started as an attempt to solve that problem for myself; a system that could track my work and accomplishments, show how they contribute to my growth and give me an actual sense of progress. Instead of reducing myself to just another resume or a list of credentials, I wanted to build something that could show the story and growth of the person behind the work.
